THE EXPERIENCE

PAGES AND KEY FEATURES

CONVERSATION

1.Chat-first payments

The Chats screen is the home base, showing pending payment requests at the top so users can settle them without opening a conversation. Inside a chat, a quick action menu lets users send or request money, share photos, locations, and voice messages, or ask the AI assistant for help. Requests can go straight to a contact or be shared as a QR code or payment link, even with people not yet on Plaza.

AI-ASSISTED PAYMENTS

2.Plaza Smart Assistant

The Plaza Smart Assistant is a built-in AI helper that users can open from any chat, either by typing or speaking. It can help pay bills, send or request money, set smart reminders, and point users to the right chat or mini-app. It always asks for confirmation before carrying out any money, order, security, or verification action. Working alongside it, Smart Amount Detection notices when an amount is mentioned in a conversation, like “₱250 lang,” and offers a one-tap option to send or request that amount, or dismiss the prompt if it wasn’t meant as a payment.

SHARED PAYMENTS

3.Group Collections

Group Collections let organizers set an amount and due date, track who has paid, and send reminders to pending members.

BUY AND SELL

4.Marketplace

The Marketplace lets users browse nearby items, chat with sellers, and pay securely, with order tracking in the same thread. Sellers can list items with up to 10 photos, save drafts, and preview listings before publishing.

PARTNER SERVICES

5.Plaza hub

The Plaza hub gathers mini-apps and partner services like entertainment, travel, hotels, bills, and government services in one place.

MONEY MANAGEMENT

6.Wallet

The Wallet shows the user's balance, top-up options, and a full transaction history, while a mascot shortcut on the Chats screen gives quick access to the wallet, notifications, and the assistant.

THE EXPERIENCE

PAGES AND KEY FEATURES

1.Customer Home and Profile

On the customer app, the Home screen shows the user’s name, points balance, and personal QR code, with an option to download it and a list of recent transactions below. Users can update their details in Edit Profile.

2.Transactions

Customers can review all earned and redeemed points in Transactions using tabs and search. The merchant Transactions screen tracks customer activity with tabs, search, and date filters like Today, Last 7 Days, or a custom range.

3.Partners

The Partners page helps customers explore participating merchants and learn each one’s earning rules, redemption options, and terms.

THE EXPERIENCE

PAGES AND KEY FEATURES

1.Sign-in and Landing Page

Tourists can sign in using an email address, phone number, or available single sign-on options. The Landing Page brings destinations, activities, and local service providers together. When a visitor opens an organizer’s link, listings are filtered to the promoted province or area; direct access shows all available locations.

2.Listings and Booking

Each Listing page provides offer descriptions, a photo gallery, inclusions, and exclusions. Visitors can check availability, choose travel dates and party size, and add optional services before placing a booking.

3.Cart and Checkout

The Cart summarizes selected destinations and activities, dates, traveler counts, and costs. Checkout & Payment lets tourists pay using a supported method and provides a confirmation with a transaction ID and reference.

4.QR Travel Pass

The QR Page displays a personal code for identification, verification, and access during a trip. Tourists can save the code for quick access or regenerate it when needed for security.

5.My Bookings

Upcoming bookings show whether a reservation is Pending Payment, Partially Paid, or Confirmed, with actions to finish payment, review the booking summary, and share trip access with companions. Past bookings let tourists rate their experience and leave a review.

6.Profile

The Profile page keeps personal account details together and provides quick access to FAQs, support, and policy information.

THE EXPERIENCE

PAGES AND KEY FEATURES

1.Dashboard and Events

The Dashboard gives organizers an overall sales summary, a ticket performance table, a sales trend chart, filters, and options to share or download reports as CSV. The Events list includes search, filters, and event cards with quick actions, while each event opens into a full workspace with navigation, status information, and management tools.

2.Create an Event

Creating an event starts with a choice between AI generation and manual setup, followed by venue selection and a dedicated details form. The guided flow gives organizers control while keeping each step focused.

3.Create Ticket Types

Organizers can build ticket types in a single table, setting names, descriptions, pricing, discounts, quantities, and other details for each ticket. A focused discount dialog makes it easy to choose a fixed amount or percentage and review the final price before saving.

4.Review and Publish

Before going live, a step-by-step review flags missing required fields, lets organizers choose a visual style for their public page, schedule its visibility, and confirm the organizer agreement before publishing.

5.Tickets

The Tickets page gives organizers searchable purchase records with ticket codes, event and ticket types, holders, amounts, and statuses. Opening a ticket reveals purchaser details, participant registration progress, and the QR code used for check-in.

6.Orders and Reservations

Orders show clear statuses with a dedicated details panel. The Reservations flow lets organizers create reservations, share payment links, and automatically release tickets if payment is not completed.

7.Check-ins and Payouts

Check-ins focus on participant records and statuses. Payouts include request tracking, proof of payment, and an event ledger for a clear record of all activity.

8.Forms

Organizers can create reusable participant registration forms from scratch or start with templates for basic registration, athlete profiles, health declarations, corporate events, feedback, and volunteer applications. The library keeps forms ready to reuse across events and ticket types.
