Jersey, lanyard, and apparel mockups that carry a brand's identity onto physical products.

A small set of merchandise design work: a full jersey kit for Chikracha, lanyard and ID holder branding for KMC, and a t-shirt mockup for Buddy Burgers, showing how a logo and color system translate onto printed and stitched goods.

Each mockup was built to show how the design reads at true scale and in context, not just as flat artwork.
