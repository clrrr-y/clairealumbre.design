GRAPHIC DESIGN

Social Media Publications

Event posters, holiday greetings, and campaign graphics designed for quick, scroll-stopping visibility.

PIECES20
CATEGORYGraphic Design
FORMATPosters & Social Cards

This collection gathers the social posts, event posters, and campaign graphics made for Asbir Tech, The Weekly Sillimanian, and various campus organizations and events at Silliman University — from pageant promos and podcast covers to seasonal greetings and tournament announcements.

Each piece is built to work first as a small, fast-scrolling image: bold typography, high-contrast color, and a single clear focal point, adapted across square and portrait formats for different platforms.
