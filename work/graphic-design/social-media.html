GRAPHIC DESIGN

Social Media

This collection brings together social posts, event posters, and campaign graphics, from pageant promos and podcast covers to seasonal greetings and tournament announcements. Every project starts with understanding the message and the audience, then finding the one visual idea that says it best.

I design each piece to work first as a small, fast-scrolling image, using bold typography, high-contrast color, and a single clear focal point. From there, I adapt it across square and portrait formats so it looks just as strong on every platform.
