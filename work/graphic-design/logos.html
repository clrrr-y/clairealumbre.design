Logo Designs

This collection brings together logos and brand marks designed for a range of businesses, products, and projects. Every logo starts with understanding who the brand is, what it stands for, and the feeling it should leave behind, then sketching ideas until one simple concept captures it.

I build each mark as a clean vector so it stays sharp at any size, from a tiny app icon to a large banner. From there, I test it in color and black and white, and across different backgrounds, so it stays recognizable wherever it appears.
