Sports

Athletes caught mid-motion — volleyball, basketball, badminton, softball, and track — across campus courts and fields.

PIECES9
CATEGORYPhotography

A set of action shots from sports played and watched on campus: a volleyball block at the net, a badminton player diving for a return, a basketball one-on-one, and a softball catcher and batter mid-play. Motion and timing carry most of these frames, whether it's a runner's legs blurred mid-stride or a row of athletes working through an agility ladder drill.

The set also includes the moments around the game itself — a team huddled together before a play, and a team celebrating and high-fiving after a match — alongside a lighter shot of a group juggling and tossing oranges outdoors.
